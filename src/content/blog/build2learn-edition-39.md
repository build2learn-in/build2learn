## 🥇 First place: Tamil live captions for educational content

**Team: Devi Prasad, Neruban, Janani, Jeshwar and Vivek**

The pitch was simple and it landed hard. There is a ridiculous amount of world-class educational video out there, and almost all of it is in English. If you're a Tamil-medium student, you're starting the race a few metres behind everyone else, for no fault of yours.

So the team built live Tamil captioning — subtitles that follow the video as it plays — so that a student can sit down with the same lecture everyone else is watching and actually _learn_ from it, not just decode it.

They had the clearest social impact in the room, they had a working demo, and they handled every question thrown at them without flinching. Easy first.

## 🥈 Second place: Sentinel X

**Team: Jaswanth, Vellaiyan, Rithikanth and Prabakaran (Ideas2IT)**

Ever looked at a wall of AWS CloudWatch logs after an incident and thought, _okay but what actually happened here?_ That's the problem Sentinel X goes after.

It pulls in the live logs, figures out the attack that happened, and then writes the **story** of that attack — where it came from, how it moved, what it touched — so the humans looking at it get a full picture instead of a haystack.

The way I'd describe it: **Jira user stories, but for an attack on your app.** Someone broke in? Here's the epic, here are the tickets. Go fix.

## 🥉 Third place: Zero Accountant

**Team: Mani and Muralidharan**

Mani is a repeat offender, in the best way. He started this app at a Build2Learn last year and came back to keep building — which, if you've been following along, is exactly the kind of thing this community exists for.

Zero Accountant is a **document-first money workspace**. Snap a picture of a bill, upload it, and the app reads it and files it away. This time round he added two things:

- **Email scanning** — it picks up bills landing in your inbox and categorises them automatically inside the app.
- **Family support** — everyone's spending, splits and settlements under one roof, with monthly reports on top.

Their tagline says it better than I can: _"Turn bills into clean records and settlements."_

Really, really useful thing. Go poke around it.
